Sharp log-Sobolev inequality on finite cyclic groups

This project uses Lean, a programming language in which a computer can verify mathematical proofs, to verify the sharp log-Sobolev inequality in Theorem 1 of:

Xinyuan Xie and Haonan Zhang,
Sharp log-Sobolev inequalities and quartic stability on finite cyclic groups,
arXiv:2606.02847.

The heat density at the origin spreads along the finite cycle until all nodes reach the same temperature. The heat tranfer dynamics here are governed by the word-length Laplacian \(-A_{\psi_n}\).

The inequality we verified here proves the optimal bound on the ratio of the relative entropy and the Dirichlet energy for that dynamics.

Mathematical statement

Let \(\mathbb Z_n=\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z\) be equipped with the uniform probability measure \(\pi\). The word-length Laplacian and its Dirichlet form are

\[\begin{aligned} A_{\psi_n}f(x)&=\sum_{k=0}^{n-1}\min(k,n-k)\widehat f(k)e^{2\pi i kx/n},\\ \mathcal E_n(f)&=\pi(\overline f\,A_{\psi_n}f), \end{aligned}\]

where \(\widehat f(k)=\frac1n\sum_{x\in\mathbb Z_n}f(x)e^{-2\pi i kx/n}\) is the Fourier transform of \(f\) and \(\pi(g)=\frac1n\sum_{x\in\mathbb Z_n}g(x)\) denotes the average of \(g\).

For \(g\ge0\), denote by \(\mathrm{Ent}_\pi(g)=\pi(g\,\log\,g)-\pi(g)\,\log\,\pi(g)\) the relative entropy of \(g\), with \(\log\) denoting the natural logarithm and \(0\,\log\,0=0\).

Theorem 1. For every \(n\geq4\) and every nonnegative \(f:\mathbb Z_n\to\mathbb R\),

\[\mathrm{Ent}_\pi(f^2)\leq2\mathcal E_n(f).\qquad(1)\]

Sharpness of the constant \(2\) follows from a standard perturbation argument (see Section 5), and we only formalize the inequality part here.
